Kelly's Coin

Support

Kelly's Coin is a short, hands-on course on position sizing: how much of your money to put into a risky investment. You play a game based on a real experiment, then try different amounts in the same rounds and see what happens. Along the way you meet the Kelly criterion, a rule for sizing investments so your money grows fastest in the long run.

Common questions

How do I start over?
Your progress is saved on your iPhone. To start again from Chapter 1, delete the app and install it again.

Is real money involved?
No. Kelly's Coin is an educational course with play money only, and nothing in it is financial advice.

What data does the app collect?
None. See the privacy policy.

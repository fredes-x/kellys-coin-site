Kelly's Coin

Privacy Policy

Effective 24 October 2026

Kelly's Coin is an iPhone game that teaches bet sizing with play money.

The short version: Kelly's Coin collects no data. Nothing you do in the app is sent to the developer or to anyone else.

What stays on your device

  • The app saves your progress: which chapters you have finished and your most recent game. It keeps this in a small file on your iPhone, and the app never sends it anywhere.
  • Deleting the app deletes this file.
  • Like other app data, the file may be included in your device backups, such as iCloud Backup. Apple handles those backups under your own Apple Account. The developer can't see them.

What the app doesn't do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No analytics, crash reporting, advertising or tracking.
  • No third-party services or SDKs that collect data.
  • No network connections: the app works entirely offline.
  • No purchases and no real money. All money in the game is play money.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
